CampusNet+ System Specification

India's Premier National Academic, Innovation & Research Network
A unified digital ecosystem connecting higher education institutions, faculty guides, PhD scholars, and students across all 28 States and 8 Union Territories.

Executive Mandate: CampusNet+ serves as an end-to-end national digital backbone for collegiate project incubation, Smart India Hackathons (SIH), geofenced attendance tracking, tamper-proof credential issuance, and inter-university research publication discovery.

1. The Problem Space in Indian Higher Education

Innovation in Indian higher education has historically been hindered by systemic fragmentation:

  • Institutional Isolation: Talented students in tier-2/tier-3 engineering colleges cannot readily partner with specialists or find research guides at premier institutes (IITs, NITs, IISc).
  • Hackathon Proxy Attendance: On-campus and inter-college hackathons struggle with manual paperwork, duplicate registration, and proxy check-ins.
  • Unverifiable Paper Certificates: Physical and standard PDF certificates of merit or participation lack machine-readable, cryptographic authentication.
  • Underutilized Academic Research: Capstone projects and student publications frequently go unindexed, preventing commercial incubation or cross-disciplinary collaboration.

Security Engineering & Anti-Fraud Mechanisms

Mathematical and cryptographic protocols preventing proxy attendance, credential falsification, and injection attacks.

1. Geofencing Mathematics: Haversine Distance Formula

To verify that participants are physically present at the venue before marking attendance, the system evaluates the great-circle distance between device GPS coordinates and the venue anchor:

a = sin²(Δφ/2) + cos(φ1) × cos(φ2) × sin²(Δλ/2)
c = 2 × atan2(√a, √(1-a))
distance = EarthRadius (6,371,000m) × c

If distance > allowedRadiusMeters, the check-in is rejected, preventing remote proxy fraud.

2. Cryptographic QR Verification & Public Lookup

Every certificate generated on CampusNet+ is assigned a unique, collision-resistant identifier and QR payload: 

Certificate ID Format: CN-[INSTITUTION]-[EVENT_CODE]-[ROLE]-[SERIAL_HASH]
Example: CN-KEC-AI26-PART-000101

QR Payload URL: https://campusnet.in/verify/CN-KEC-AI26-PART-000101

3. Strict URL Allowlists & File Sanitization (validation.ts)

  • Domain Allowlists: External profile URLs are strictly validated against github.com, linkedin.com, x.com, leetcode.com, and vidwan.inflibnet.ac.in.
  • File Upload Defense: 10 MB size limit, PDF/image MIME enforcement, path traversal stripping (../), and blocking of executable formats (.exe, .sh, .php, .js).
